# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to make Newsletter Flow a practical and respectful project for writers, builders, and maintainers.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, kind, and specific.
- Assume good intent while still naming concrete problems.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Respect privacy, especially around drafts, sources, and unpublished writing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private drafts, keys, tokens, or identifying information.
- Repeatedly derailing technical or product discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove issues, comments, or contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a ban from the project.

Security-sensitive reports should use:
